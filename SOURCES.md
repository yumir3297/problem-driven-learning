# Sources and Rationale

Research is used as design guidance, not as a claim that one teaching sequence is universally optimal.

## Agent Skill format and runtime discovery

1. OpenAI, **Build skills** — https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills?product=breeze
   - Skill folder with `SKILL.md`, required metadata, and optional supporting files.
   - Progressive disclosure: metadata is discoverable first; supporting content can be loaded as needed.
2. OpenCode, **Agent Skills** — https://opencode.ai/docs/skills
   - Documents `SKILL.md` structure and discovery from personal and project directories, including `~/.agents/skills/`.
3. OpenAI Codex, **Agent Skills** — https://developers.openai.ac.cn/codex/skills
   - Documents Codex personal skill location and manual skill creation.

## Learning science

1. Dunlosky, J., Rawson, K. A., Marsh, E. J., Nathan, M. J., & Willingham, D. T. (2013). *Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: Promising Directions From Cognitive and Educational Psychology*. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266
   - Practice testing and distributed practice received high-utility assessments in the review; self-explanation and interleaving received more qualified assessments.
2. Roediger, H. L., & Karpicke, J. D. (2006). *Test-Enhanced Learning: Taking Memory Tests Improves Long-Term Retention*. Psychological Science, 17(3), 249–255.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x
   - Retrieval tests improved delayed retention relative to repeated study in the reported experiments.
3. Chi, M. T. H., de Leeuw, N., Chiu, M.-H., & LaVancher, C. (1994). *Eliciting Self-Explanations Improves Understanding*. Cognitive Science, 18(3), 439–477.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1803_3
   - Supports prompting learners to explain connections and reasoning, while noting the original study's context and sample.
4. Atkinson, R. K., Derry, S. J., Renkl, A., & Wortham, D. (2000). *Learning from Examples: Instructional Principles from the Worked Examples Research*. Review of Educational Research, 70(2), 181–214.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543070002181
   - Reviews design principles for worked examples, including conceptual structure, self-explanation, and proximity to matched practice.
5. Camp, G. et al. (2026). *A Meta-Analysis of the Effectiveness and Boundary Conditions of Retrieval Practice, Distributed Practice, and Interleaved Practice in Authentic School Classrooms*.
   - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-026-10225-5
   - Reports average effects in authentic school classrooms while emphasizing moderators and boundary conditions. This supports adaptive rather than one-size-fits-all use.

## Practical interpretation

- Ask learners to retrieve, explain, and apply rather than only reread.
- Use worked examples when a learner lacks the prerequisites for productive independent problem solving.
- Fade support as the learner demonstrates competence.
- Space review when long-term retention matters, but choose timing and format based on the learning goal.
- Do not treat a research finding as a universal rule for every subject, learner, or lesson.
